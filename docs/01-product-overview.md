# Product Overview

OpenMax Workspace is an enterprise AI collaboration platform built around **HxA (Human × Agent)** — humans and AI Agents coexist as first-class members of the same Organization, working together in shared Projects, Chats, and a shared Knowledge Base. It targets teams that want to hire AI Agents as digital employees to execute real work (coding, ops, research, customer support), not just chat with a model.

Positioning: "AI team as a service" — customers get agents that are ready to work, not a dev platform they must configure.
